What to Feed a Pomeranian?

Expert nutrition advice for American dog owners

← Back to Pomeranian Names

The Short Answer: High-Quality Kibble with Real Meat

Your Pom needs balanced, premium food with real meat as the first ingredient. Skip fillers like corn, wheat, or soy—Poms have specific needs thanks to their small size and big energy.

Choosing the Right Food

  • Dry Kibble: Look for brands with chicken, turkey, or fish first on the list.
  • Wet Food: Mix with kibble for moisture and flavor—great for picky eaters!
  • Raw/Home-Cooked: If you go this route, work with your vet to get the balance right.

Feeding by Life Stage

Puppies (0-12 months)

Puppies need extra calories for growth. Feed 3-4 times a day with puppy-specific food. Follow the package guide based on their weight.

Adults (1-7 years)

Most adult Poms do well with 2 meals a day. Typically ¼ to ½ cup total—divided into two servings.

Seniors (7+ years)

Senior Poms may need fewer calories but more joint support. Look for senior formulas with glucosamine.

Foods to Never Feed Your Pom

Some human foods are toxic—keep these away:

  • Chocolate, coffee, caffeine
  • Grapes or raisins
  • Onions, garlic, chives
  • Avocado
  • Alcohol
  • Xylitol (artificial sweetener)
  • Macadamia nuts

Feeding Tips from American Owners

  • Stick to a Routine: Feed at the same times every day.
  • Watch Portions: Poms love food and can easily become overweight.
  • Fresh Water Always: Keep their bowl full of clean water.
  • Treats in Moderation: Treats should be less than 10% of their daily calories.

Final Thoughts

Feeding your Pomeranian a balanced, high-quality diet keeps them healthy and happy. Check in with your vet regularly—they can help adjust portions as your Pom ages or if health issues pop up.
