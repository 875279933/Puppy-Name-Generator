How to Train a Pomeranian Puppy?

Expert training advice for American dog owners

← Back to Pomeranian Names

The Short Answer: Start Early with Positive Reinforcement

Pomeranian puppies are smart and love to please, making training fun! Start around 8-12 weeks using treats, praise, and lots of affection.

Basic Commands to Teach First

  • Sit: Hold a treat above their head and say "Sit." Reward when they sit.
  • Stay: Hold your hand out, say "Stay," and reward for staying.
  • Come: Call their name plus "Come" and reward when they come.
  • Down: Lower a treat to the ground and say "Down."
  • Leave It: Teach them to ignore things on the floor.

Potty Training Tips

  • Take them out first thing in the morning, after meals, naps, and play.
  • Pick one spot in your yard and always use it.
  • Use a command like "Go potty" so they learn the routine.
  • Reward immediately when they go outside—they need to make the connection!

Crate Training

Crate training helps with potty training and keeps them safe. Make the crate cozy with blankets and treats. Never use it as punishment—keep it positive!

Socialization Matters

Introduce your Pom to new people, dogs, sounds, and places early on. This builds confidence and prevents fearfulness later.

Common Training Challenges

  • Barking: Teach "Quiet" by waiting for them to stop, then reward.
  • Chewing: Give them plenty of chew toys and redirect when they chew wrong things.
  • Separation Anxiety: Gradually get them used to being alone for short periods.

Training Tips for Success

  • Keep sessions short—5-10 minutes is enough.
  • Be consistent with commands and rewards.
  • Stay patient—accidents happen, especially with puppies!
  • Use high-value treats for training (small pieces work best).

What American Owners Say

Final Thoughts

Training your Pomeranian puppy strengthens your bond and makes them a well-behaved family member. With patience and positivity, your Pom will learn quickly and become the perfect companion.
