How Long Do Weiner Dogs Get?

An honest size and growth guide for American dachshund owners, from puppy to full-grown

The Quick Answer

A full-grown standard dachshund usually measures 8 to 9 inches tall at the shoulder and 16 to 22 inches long from chest to rump. Miniature dachshunds are noticeably smaller at 5 to 6 inches tall and 10 to 14 inches long. The long body is exactly what earned them the "wiener dog" nickname that American families have used for over a hundred years.

Why "Weiner Dog" Is a Fair Description

The math really does work out. A standard dachshund's body is roughly 2 to 2.5 times longer than it is tall. Add in the short legs, deep chest, and low-slung belly, and you have the hot-dog shape that the name suggests. Most U.S. owners use "wiener dog," "Doxie," and "sausage dog" interchangeably, and none of them are wrong.

Standard Dachshund Size

Standards are the larger of the two American varieties. The AKC puts them at 16 to 32 pounds, with most adults landing between 18 and 26 pounds. Length runs from about 16 inches in a smaller female to 22 inches in a long, lean male.

Standard Measurements at a Glance

  • Height: 8 to 9 inches at the shoulder
  • Length: 16 to 22 inches nose to rump
  • Weight: 16 to 32 pounds
  • Growth plate: 10 to 12 months

Standards finish growing a bit later than minis, and some males keep filling out until 14 months. If you are picking one of the dachshund puppy names from our generator for a standard, expect the name to suit a slightly bigger, sturdier dog.

Miniature Dachshund Size

Minis are what most American city dwellers picture when they say "wiener dog." Tiny, tidy, and easy to tuck under one arm. They top out at 11 pounds according to the AKC breed standard, with most adults between 8 and 11 pounds.

Miniature Measurements at a Glance

  • Height: 5 to 6 inches at the shoulder
  • Length: 10 to 14 inches nose to rump
  • Weight: under 11 pounds
  • Growth plate: 8 to 10 months

Minis reach their full size faster. A 6-month-old miniature is usually within a pound of its adult weight, while standards can keep growing for another six months.

Growth Chart by Month

The chart below reflects what most U.S. breeders and vets see. Real dogs can fall outside these numbers, especially in mixed-line breedings.

Standard Dachshund Growth

  • 8 weeks: 3 to 5 pounds, 5 to 6 inches tall
  • 12 weeks: 6 to 9 pounds, 6 to 7 inches tall
  • 6 months: 12 to 18 pounds, 7 to 8 inches tall
  • 12 months: 16 to 26 pounds, 8 to 9 inches tall

Miniature Dachshund Growth

  • 8 weeks: 1.5 to 3 pounds, 3 to 4 inches tall
  • 12 weeks: 3 to 4.5 pounds, 4 to 5 inches tall
  • 6 months: 6 to 9 pounds, 5 to 6 inches tall
  • 12 months: 8 to 11 pounds, 5 to 6 inches tall

What Affects How Long a Weiner Dog Gets

Genetics sets the range, but a few day-to-day factors can stretch or shrink the final size.

Diet and Weight

Overfed dachshunds look longer simply because they carry more belly. Lean, well-muscled dogs hold the natural shape of the breed. Obesity in dachshunds is a real problem in the United States. The Association for Pet Obesity Prevention has put the breed in its "at risk" category for years.

Spay and Neuter Timing

U.S. studies suggest dogs fixed before 6 months sometimes grow a touch taller than those fixed later. It is a small effect, but it can make a mini look almost standard-sized if the surgery is done too early.

Coat Type

Coat does not change length, but it changes how the length looks. Longhaired dachshunds appear softer and sometimes visually longer, while smooths show off every inch of their shape. Wirehairs tend to look stockier.

How to Measure Your Dachshund at Home

You only need a soft tape measure and a flat surface.

  1. Have your dog stand on a hard floor, head up, looking forward.
  2. Measure from the ground to the top of the shoulder blades for height.
  3. Measure from the point of the chest to the back of the hips for length.
  4. Use a baby scale or your vet's scale for weight.

This is the same method American show handlers use, and it is accurate enough for tracking growth at home.

When to Worry About Size

A miniature dachshund over 12 pounds, or a standard under 14 pounds at full maturity, may not match the breed standard. That is fine for a pet, but it can matter if you plan to show the dog. More importantly, a sudden drop in weight or a "longer than usual" look in an adult can sometimes signal a back issue. A quick vet check is worth it if you see rapid changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do miniature dachshunds get?

Most miniature dachshunds reach 10 to 14 inches from chest to rump at maturity, which is about half the length of a standard. They also weigh less, usually under 11 pounds.

Do all dachshunds have the same long shape?

Yes. The long body and short legs are part of the breed standard for every variety. Some dogs are slightly longer or shorter than others, but the general proportions stay the same.

At what age is a dachshund fully grown?

Miniatures usually reach full size by 10 to 12 months. Standards can keep filling out until 12 to 14 months, especially males. Growth plates are typically closed by 12 months, which is also the age when most American vets clear the dog for higher-impact activities like agility.

Final Thoughts

How long do weiner dogs get? Long enough that the name fits perfectly. Standards run 16 to 22 inches, minis run 10 to 14 inches, and every one of them has the same unmistakable shape that has charmed American families since the 1800s.
