How Much Are Bichon Frise Puppies?

Current prices and what to expect when buying a Bichon Frise puppy in America

← Back to Bichon Frise Names

Average Price Range

As of 2024, the average price for a Bichon Frise puppy from a reputable breeder in the United States ranges from $1,500 to $3,000. However, prices can vary significantly based on several factors we'll explore below.

What Affects the Price?

Breeder Reputation and Experience

Established breeders with years of experience and positive reviews typically charge more. These breeders invest in health testing, proper socialization, and quality care for their puppies. Expect to pay $2,000-$3,000 from a top-tier breeder.

Bloodlines and Pedigree

Puppies with champion bloodlines or show potential command higher prices. If you're looking for a pet-quality Bichon, you can expect to pay less than for a show-quality puppy.

Geographic Location

Prices vary across the country:

  • East Coast: $1,800-$3,000 (higher in NYC, Boston)
  • West Coast: $2,000-$3,500 (especially California)
  • Midwest: $1,500-$2,500 (more affordable)
  • South: $1,500-$2,800

Age and Availability

Younger puppies (8-12 weeks) are usually more expensive than older puppies. Rare colors or limited availability can also drive up prices.

What You Get for Your Money

A reputable breeder should provide:

  • Health certificate from a licensed vet
  • Up-to-date vaccinations
  • De-worming treatments
  • Microchip
  • AKC registration papers (for purebred puppies)
  • Health guarantee
  • Socialization with people and other animals
  • Support and guidance for new owners

Red Flags for Low Prices

Be cautious if you see Bichon Frise puppies priced significantly below the average:

  • Under $1,000: May indicate a puppy mill or irresponsible breeding
  • No health records: Avoid breeders who can't provide vet documentation
  • Unwilling to show facilities: Reputable breeders welcome visits
  • Multiple litters available: Could be a commercial breeding operation

Adoption vs. Buying from a Breeder

Adoption is a great alternative for those who want to give a Bichon Frise a second chance:

  • Adoption fees: $300-$600
  • Includes spay/neuter, vaccinations, and microchip
  • Many rescues have puppies and young dogs available

Additional Costs to Consider

Remember that the puppy price is just the beginning. You'll also need to budget for:

  • Initial supplies (crate, bed, food bowls, toys): $300-$500
  • Veterinary care (wellness exams, vaccinations): $500-$1,000
  • Training classes: $100-$300
  • Spay/neuter if not already done: $200-$400

Where to Find Bichon Frise Puppies

Here are reliable sources to find Bichon Frise puppies:

  • AKC Marketplace: Verified breeders with AKC-registered puppies
  • Bichon Frise Club of America: Breeder directory
  • Local breed clubs: Referrals from experienced owners
  • Rescue organizations: Bichon Frise Rescue groups across the country

Conclusion

While Bichon Frise puppies aren't cheap, they're a lifelong investment in companionship. Expect to pay $1,500-$3,000 from a reputable breeder, and remember to factor in ongoing expenses. Whether you choose to buy from a breeder or adopt, a Bichon Frise will bring joy and love to your home for years to come.
