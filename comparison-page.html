How to Compare Puppy Names

Use this page to review names you saved from our puppy name generator. Click the heart icon on any name to add it to your shortlist, then open this page to compare meanings and popularity side by side.

What to Look For When Comparing

  • Length and clarity. Shorter names with one or two syllables are easier for dogs to recognize during training.
  • Meaning. Each name card shows its origin and meaning so you can pick one that fits your puppy's personality.
  • Popularity. Star ratings reflect how common each name is, based on trends from the American Kennel Club and pet registries.
