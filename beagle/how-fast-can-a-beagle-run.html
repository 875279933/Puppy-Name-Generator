How Fast Can a Beagle Run?

Spoiler: These little hounds are faster than you think!

← Back to Generator

The Short Answer: Up to 20-25 MPH!

Beagles might look like cuddly little couch potatoes, but don’t let that fool you—they’re surprisingly fast! A healthy adult Beagle can hit speeds of 20-25 mph, which is faster than Bulldogs, Pugs, and even some Retrievers. Who knew these floppy-eared cuties had such a need for speed?

Why Are Beagles Built for Speed?

Let’s not forget—Beagles were bred as hunting hounds, chasing down rabbits and other small game. They needed speed and stamina to keep up! Here’s what makes ’em so quick:

  • Muscular Little Bodies: Those short legs are packed with muscle—perfect for sprinting!
  • Lightweight: At 20-30 pounds, they’re light enough to accelerate super fast.
  • Stamina for Days: They can keep going and going—great for long hunts (or long walks!).
  • Prey Drive: When they catch a scent? Watch out—nothing stops a Beagle on a mission!

How Do They Compare to Other Dogs?

Curious how Beagles measure up against other popular breeds? Check this out:

  • German Shepherd: 30-35 mph (faster)
  • Golden Retriever: 20-25 mph (same as Beagle!)
  • Labrador Retriever: 20-25 mph (tie!)
  • Dachshund: 15-20 mph (slower)
  • Bulldog: 10-15 mph (way slower)

How Much Exercise Does a Beagle Actually Need?

These energetic little dogs need regular exercise to stay happy (and out of trouble!). Here’s what they really need:

Daily Walks Are a Must

Aim for 30-60 minutes of walking every day. Beagles love sniffing around, so mix up your routes—they’ll thank you for the new smells!

Let ’Em Run Free (Safely!)

Find a fenced yard or dog park where your Beagle can stretch their legs. They’ll go crazy for a chance to run wild!

Playtime = Happy Beagle

Fetch, tug-of-war, or scent games—whatever gets them moving! Mental stimulation is just as important as physical exercise.

Agility Training Is Fun for Both of You

Beagles are smart and love a challenge. Agility courses are a great way to bond and burn off energy.

Safety First! Tips for Exercising Your Beagle

Keep these tips in mind when you’re out and about with your speedy pup:

  • Watch the weather: Skip the walks when it’s super hot or freezing—Beagles aren’t fans of extreme temps.
  • Bring water: Always have a water bowl handy for both of you—they’ll get thirsty fast!
  • Check those paws: After walks, check for cuts or little rocks stuck between their toes.
  • Start slow: If your Beagle is new to exercise, don’t go all-out right away—build up gradually.
  • Use a harness: A good harness is way safer than a collar for active Beagles (no choking risk!).

So, Are Beagles Fast?

Absolutely! These little hounds can hit 20-25 mph when they want to—and they’ve got the stamina to keep going. If you’re looking for an active, fun-loving companion, a Beagle might be perfect for you. Just be ready to keep up!
