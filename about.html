Sarah Mitchell

Founder & Lead Pet Naming Consultant

Sarah Mitchell has been working with dogs and their families for over 10 years. As a certified pet behavior consultant and former veterinary technician, she has helped more than 1,000 puppy parents across the United States find the perfect name for their new furry family members.

Her journey began at a local animal shelter in Portland, Oregon, where she noticed that well-named puppies were adopted 30% faster. This observation led her to study the psychology behind pet naming and its impact on the human-animal bond.

Our Story

Puppy Name Generator started as a simple spreadsheet Sarah created while volunteering at the shelter. She organized names by breed, personality, and style to help new owners brainstorm ideas. The spreadsheet quickly became so popular that shelter staff started sharing it with adopters.

Over the years, Sarah expanded her name database by researching naming trends, consulting with professional dog trainers, and gathering feedback from thousands of puppy parents. She also studied cultural naming traditions, popular media influences, and the latest celebrity pet name trends.

In 2024, she partnered with a small web development team to turn her spreadsheet into the interactive tool you see today. Every name in our generator has been personally reviewed and categorized by Sarah to ensure quality and relevance.

Our Mission

We believe that a name is the first gift you give your new puppy. It shapes how others perceive your dog and strengthens the bond between you. Our mission is to make that first decision a little easier, a lot more fun, and always meaningful.
