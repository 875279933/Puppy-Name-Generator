About Pug Dogs

Pugs are one of the most beloved small breeds in America. With their wrinkled faces, curled tails, and big round eyes, they have a look that is impossible to resist. These compact little dogs typically weigh between 14 and 18 pounds, making them perfect for apartment living and city life.

Why Pugs Make Great Pets

Pugs are known for their easygoing nature. They get along well with kids, seniors, and other pets. Their exercise needs are modest — a short walk and some playtime usually do the trick. They are also surprisingly expressive, using a range of snorts, grunts, and happy wiggles to communicate.

Choosing the Perfect Pug Dog Name

When picking a name for your pug, consider their unique traits. Their round, squishy appearance inspires food-themed names like Mochi, Peanut, or Waffles. Their comical personality fits names like Tank, Gizmo, or Pugsley. And their stocky build pairs well with short, punchy names like Otis, Gus, or Milo.

More Tips for New Pug Owners

Pugs require regular face cleaning to keep their wrinkles free of dirt and moisture. Daily wiping and weekly baths go a long way. They also enjoy short walks and indoor play. With proper care and attention, your pug will be a loyal and loving companion for many years. Compare your favorite names to find the perfect fit!

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the most popular names for pugs?

Otis, Bruno, Luna, and Stella are favorites. Many owners pick short, chunky names that match a pug's compact build. Food-inspired picks like Mochi and Peanut are also common.

How do I choose the right name for my pug?

Think about your puppy's personality and appearance. Wrinkly faces and curled tails inspire names like Tank or Gizmo. Food-themed options like Mochi or Peanut work well too.

Are pug names gender-specific?

Some are traditionally male or female, but many owners go gender-neutral. Popular male picks include Otis, Bruno, and Milo. Female favorites include Luna, Stella, and Bella.

How can I test if a name fits my pug?

Say it out loud a few times. Call your puppy by it during playtime and meals to see how they respond. You can also use the Try Compare feature to view options side by side.
